SLEAZEBURG 64

A top-down crime satire in the style of an 8-bit home computer game: 320×200 pixels, 16 colours, sprites and three-voice sound. Walk, drive, take jobs from the flashing J markers, dodge the law, and raise $25,000 to buy the election.

An original game. Every place, business and official in Sleazeburg is made up.

CONTROLS

Arrows / WASD
Walk, drive
Space
Fire or punch on foot, horn in a car
E / Enter
Get in or out of a car
M
Music on or off (starts off)
R / N
Next radio station / next tune
F
Sound effects on or off
P
Pause
I / Esc
Open or close this page

RADIO

The radio plays on the title screen and while you drive. Tunes are real Commodore 64 music, streamed on demand from the High Voltage SID Collection and played by a SID chip emulator in your browser. Emulation is approximate, so some tunes sound different from the real hardware.
