homelab

A lab for learning. Segmented network, apps only over VPN.

Storage, apps, identity and monitoring all run on one box. That is a single point of failure, and I'd build it differently with a bigger budget. There is also a Kubernetes cluster. Things change here all the time, so this page shows how the lab is built, not what state it is in today.

principles

How it is run

  • Built to learn. I try things here, break them and rebuild them.
  • VPN only. No app is published to the internet.
  • SSO on everything. Every app is behind the reverse proxy and Authentik.
  • Isolated DMZ. No LAN access, external DNS only, limited egress.
  • Allow-listed egress. The DMZ and the AI stack only reach what they need.
  • Everything in Git. Compose stacks, manifests and runbooks are in Forgejo, and work is tracked as issues there.
  • Real backups. Snapshots, encrypted replication to another machine, and restore tests.
  • Secrets in OpenBao. Apps get their own short-lived access instead of env files.
  • Works offline. Important repos are mirrored locally, and Wikipedia and iFixit are available through Kiwix.
  • Monitoring in one place. Metrics, logs and security events are collected centrally, and alerts go out through ntfy.
