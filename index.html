homelab

A learning lab: hard-walled network, apps reachable only over a mesh VPN

One platform box carries storage, apps, identity and observability. That is a single point of failure, and it is a budget decision, not the architecture I would choose. A Kubernetes cluster runs alongside it. Like any homelab, it is always in a state of flux, so read this page as a sketch of how things are built, not as a status report.

principles

How it is run

  • Built to learn. Things get tried, broken and rebuilt here, and the trade-offs are written down honestly.
  • VPN or nothing. No app is published to the internet; every service is reached over the mesh VPN.
  • SSO in front of everything. Every app sits behind the reverse proxy and the identity provider.
  • The DMZ is a hard wall. No LAN access, external DNS only, limited egress with narrow per-host exceptions.
  • Egress is allow-listed. The DMZ and the AI stack can only reach out to what they need.
  • Git is the source of truth. Compose stacks, manifests and runbooks live in Forgejo, with work tracked as issues.
  • Redundancy is not a backup. Snapshots, encrypted replication to separate hardware, and scheduled restore tests.
  • Secrets out of config. Apps get them from OpenBao with short-lived, per-app access.
  • Observe, then alert. Metrics, logs and security events in one place, with notifications that mean something.
